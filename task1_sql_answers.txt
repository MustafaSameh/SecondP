Q3:
    Query:
        """
        SELECT title , COUNT(b.book_id) AS Number_of_times_books_were_borrowed
        FROM books b 
        JOIN checkouts ch ON 
        b.book_id = ch.book_id 
        GROUP BY title 
        ORDER BY Number_of_times_books_were_borrowed  
        DESC LIMIT 5;
        """
    Result:
        title	            Borrowing_count
        0	The Silver Kite	             57
        1	Fossils and Fireflies	     55
