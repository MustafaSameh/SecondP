Q3 What are the most popular books?:
    Query:
        """
        SELECT title , COUNT(b.book_id) AS Borrowing_count
        FROM books b 
        JOIN checkouts ch ON 
        b.book_id = ch.book_id 
        GROUP BY title 
        ORDER BY Borrowing_count  
        DESC LIMIT 5;
        """
    Result:

Q5 What does a neighborhood's activity look like further back in time?:
    Query:
        run("""
        SELECT first_name , COUNT(book_id) AS Borrowing_count , neighborhood , checkout_date
        FROM members m
        JOIN checkouts ch 
        ON m.member_id = ch.member_id 
        WHERE neighborhood ='Maadi'
        GROUP BY first_name
        ORDER BY checkout_date DESC 
        LIMIT 10 OFFSET 11
        """)
